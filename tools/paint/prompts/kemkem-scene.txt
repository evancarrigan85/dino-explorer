The attached picture is a 3D reconstruction of the Kem Kem rivers, south-east Morocco, about 100–95 million years ago, seen from the sky.
Repaint it as ONE oil painting. Painted in the manner of Charles R. Knight (1874–1953), the American natural-history muralist: oil on canvas, confident visible brushwork, warm golden naturalistic light, soft atmospheric haze, a palette of ochres, siennas, olive greens and dusty blues, calm and dignified realism. Keep his warmth and his eye for the real world, but follow TODAY's science, not the science of his time: paint the land, water, plants and climate exactly as described here.
Keep the composition EXACTLY: the river and its sandbanks, the low red sandstone face of the far bank, every monkey-puzzle tree, and every animal where it is and as large as it is (the dark shapes are the animals).
Paint the dark shapes as the living animals, as reconstructed today: Carcharodontosaurus: a giant two-legged meat-eater with a long, deep skull and blade-like serrated teeth, short arms with three fingers, powerful hind legs, and a long tail held level off the ground; scaly skin; its colours are not known, so muted earth tones; Deltadromeus: a slender, long-legged meat-eater with long shins built for running, small arms and a long tail held level; known from one partial skeleton, and how it is related to other dinosaurs is argued over; scaly skin; its colours are not known, so muted earth tones; Spinosaurus: a long, low theropod with short hind legs, a long narrow crocodile-like snout with cone-shaped teeth, a tall sail of long spines along its back, and a deep, paddle-like tail with tall spines above and below; scaly skin; its colours are not known, so muted earth tones. The small shapes under the water are fish; the shapes in the air are pterosaurs.
Land: a low, level far bank of pale sand and reddish sandstone; a wide, flat near shore of sand and small pebbles. Water: a broad, shallow, slow river, brownish-green, braided around a pale sandbank island. Plants: a few tall conifers related to monkey-puzzle trees (straight bare trunks, branches in tiers near the top) spaced along the far bank; low leafy shrubs and small plants at the water’s edge; a few ferns. Light: a hot, hazy dry-season afternoon under a pale sky. Do not paint: grass or grassland, palm trees, cycads, broadleaf forest, flowers in bloom, cliffs, mountains, volcanoes.
