A richly detailed painting of a landscape seen from directly above. Painted in the manner of Charles R. Knight (1874–1953), the American natural-history muralist: oil on canvas, confident visible brushwork, warm golden naturalistic light, soft atmospheric haze, a palette of ochres, siennas, olive greens and dusty blues, calm and dignified realism. Keep his warmth and his eye for the real world, but follow TODAY's science, not the science of his time: paint the land, water, plants and climate exactly as described here. Fine, careful texture everywhere: grains and small pebbles on the gravel bars, ripple marks left by the current on the low sand, darker damp sand along every edge of the water, silt swirls and sand waves on the bed of the shallow river showing through brownish-green water, layered reddish sandstone on the face of the far bank, a few small low green plants near the water.
The attached picture is the guide map of this place, seen from straight above. Keep EVERY boundary exactly where it is in the guide: blue is water (darker blue is deeper), beige is sand, the darker grey-brown patches are gravel bars, the red band is the sandstone face of the far bank, the light brown above it is the level top of the far bank. Do not move, add or remove any river channel, bank, island or bar. Repaint everything: do not copy the guide's flat colours.
The place: south-east Morocco, about 100–95 million years ago.
Land: a low, level far bank of pale sand and reddish sandstone; a wide, flat near shore of sand and small pebbles.
Water: a broad, shallow, slow river, brownish-green, braided around a pale sandbank island.
Light: a hot, hazy dry-season afternoon under a pale sky.
Do not paint: grass or grassland, palm trees, cycads, broadleaf forest, flowers in bloom, cliffs, mountains, volcanoes.
Seen from straight above only: no horizon, no sky, no perspective. NO trees or tall plants (they are added separately), no tree shadows, NO animals of any kind, no footprints, no people, no buildings, no text, labels, border or frame.
